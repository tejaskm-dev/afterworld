# Sound sources

Public-domain (CC0 1.0) recordings that `tools/gen_sfx.py` reworks into Afterworld's impacts,
creature voices and interface sounds. Nothing here ships as it is: every sound in the mod is pitched,
layered, filtered and given its own space first.

| Folder | Pack | Author | Licence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `kenney_impact` | Impact Sounds 1.0 | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds |
| `kenney_rpg` | RPG Audio | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio |
| `kenney_interface` | Interface Sounds | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds |
| `oga_creatures` | 80 CC0 Creature SFX | rubberduck | CC0 1.0 | https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx |
