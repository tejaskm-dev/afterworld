Afterworld Shaders
A light, fast shader pack made for Afterworld: soft sun shadows, a warm torch glow, a painted sky,
swaying plants, living water, fog that melts into the sky, sun rays and bloom, and a gentle filmic
grade. Profiles: Lite (no shadows), Balanced, Full. Open Iris's settings (Video Settings > Shader
Packs > Afterworld Shaders > Settings) to choose one or tune each option.
